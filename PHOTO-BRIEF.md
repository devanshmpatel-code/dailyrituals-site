# Photo brief: editing the pictures so they sit together on the site

The site softens every photo with a shared tone (the "Photo grade" switch in the preview bar). That is a stand-in. For the best result, edit the originals once to one look.

**Tone and colour**
- One white balance for the whole set: slightly warm (about 5200 to 5600 K), no green or blue casts.
- Lift the shadows a little and pull the highlights down, so nothing is crushed black or blown white. Pictures sit on linen, sand, sage and plum, and harsh contrast fights them.
- Gentle saturation (about -5 to -10 percent from a neutral edit), with warm amber and glass tones left alive.
- Add a very light, even film grain if you like, to match the texture on the site.

**Backdrops**
- Shoot or edit onto soft, matte backdrops from the palette: linen (#F7F2EA), sand (#EFE6D8), sage (#E1EAE5), blush (#F1DDD5). Avoid pure white and pure black.
- Keep one backdrop colour per mood so a row of products looks like a family.
- Night scenes: deep aubergine (#2A1F33) with a single warm candle glow, not black.

**Crops and shapes (the site cuts pictures into arches and leaf shapes)**
- Subject centred, with generous head space above it (about 15 percent), because arch tops trim the corners.
- Supply 4:5 portrait (product cards, wall), 1:1 (moods), and 16:10 landscape (banners). Export at 2000 px on the long edge, JPEG quality 82 to 88.
- Leave a calm, empty edge on the side of any picture that fades into a card (the Discovery Kit photo fades on its right).

**Cut-outs (optional, best blend of all)**
- For hero products, a transparent-background PNG or WebP of the product with its soft shadow lets it sit directly on a tinted card or the sky.

**Names and delivery**
- Name files like `roller-cabana-4x5.jpg` and upload to the Wix media library; keep the originals untouched.
