Scent and the practice

Any familiar scent can be your cue

Claire's method uses a familiar scent as the cue for a calm pause. Meet the same scent at the same moment each day, and in time it becomes your reminder to stop, breathe and choose.

Nothing to buy to begin. You can use any scent you already have: your morning tea, a hand cream, a favourite soap.

If you would like a scent kept just for your pause, Claire makes one in three forms. An optional cue kit, separate from coaching.
