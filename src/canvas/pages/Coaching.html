Frequently asked

Coaching is not therapy or medical care. It does not diagnose or treat any condition and is not a substitute for care from a qualified professional. Claire to confirm wording.

Where do sessions happen?

By video, so you can join from anywhere.

Do I need to buy products?

No. The shop and coaching are separate. Some clients like using a familiar scent as a cue for their practice, but it is optional.

What if I need to reschedule?
